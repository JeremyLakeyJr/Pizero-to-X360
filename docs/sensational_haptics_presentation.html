The Name

🧠 "Sensational"

The word "sensational" has a double meaning: it refers to sensation — the physical act of feeling — and to something extraordinary or headline-worthy. Our gloves let players literally sense the virtual world, and we believe the experience is nothing short of sensational.

✋ "Haptics"

Haptics is the science of touch-based feedback. Calling ourselves "Haptics" signals that we are specialists in this space — not just another VR accessory maker, but a company whose entire identity is built around mastering the sense of touch.

🔗 Together

Sensational Haptics is easy to remember, clearly describes what we do, and inspires excitement. It positions our brand at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and unforgettable human experience.

The Slogan
"Feel Every Virtual World."

Why "Feel"?

Touch is the one sense VR has failed to recreate — until now. "Feel" is an immediate, visceral verb that instantly communicates our value proposition: we give players the sense of touch back.

The Product
Open-Source VR Haptic Gloves
ESP32 MCU ERM / LRA Vibration Motors Infrared Finger Tracking Flex Sensors BLE 5.0 Wireless USB-C Charging Meta Compatible SteamVR Compatible PSVR2 Compatible Vive Compatible Open Hardware License Community Mod Support

Purpose

Restore the sense of touch in virtual reality. Current VR controllers provide buttons and vibration — our gloves provide nuanced, localized haptic sensations on every finger.

Functions

  • Per-finger vibrotactile feedback
  • IR-based hand & finger tracking
  • Flex-sensor grip detection
  • USB-C + BLE 5.0 connectivity
  • Open SDK for custom haptic patterns
